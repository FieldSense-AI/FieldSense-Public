# Team

## Who built this

FieldSense AI is a **student-led engineering and research project** developed by
**Neha Priya** and **Lovshik Vinnu**, students of Electronics & Communication
Engineering at **Mahindra University**.

The two of us designed, built, programmed, broke and repaired every part of it:
the hardware selection and integration, the firmware, the measurement pipeline,
the on-device intelligence, the interface, and the field testing.

## How it was built

This is a prototype built the way student projects actually get built: by
ordering parts that turned out to have different pinouts than advertised, wiring
things wrong, discovering that two subsystems both wanted the same pin, and
walking outside repeatedly to find out that what worked on a bench does not
necessarily work in a field.

Some of that is recorded in [Validation](validation.md), including a full day's
field walk that was thrown away because of an arithmetic error that every
component test had passed straight through. We have kept those failures visible
rather than presenting only the runs that worked, because the failures are where
most of the engineering actually happened.
